Advanced Statistics

Lecture Notes, Videos and Learning Resources

Instructor: Julio Magaña Area: Mathematics and Statistics Tools: R and RStudio

Course Overview

This course develops fundamental and advanced concepts in probability and mathematical statistics. It introduces random variables, probability distributions, moment generating functions, statistical estimation, and analysis of variance.

Emphasis is placed on mathematical reasoning, statistical methods, and computational implementation using R.

Course Contents

  • Unit 1. Probability and Random Variables
  • Unit 2. Discrete Distributions
  • Unit 3. Continuous Distributions
  • Unit 4. Moments and Generating Functions
  • Unit 5. Point Estimation
  • Unit 6. Estimation Methods
  • Unit 7. Analysis of Variance

Fundamental concepts of probability and the mathematical definition of random variables.

Topics

  • Probability mass and density functions
  • Cumulative distribution functions
  • Expected value and variance
